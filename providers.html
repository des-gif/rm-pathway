The idea

A Practical Leadership Pipeline — Built in Partnership

Many providers have excellent senior support workers, team leaders, deputy managers and assistant managers who show the right values, maturity and commitment — but who aren't yet fully ready to step into a Registered Manager role. The partnership lets you identify those people and place them onto the pathway for structured development, preparation and future placement.

50/50

An Equal Share of Every Successful Placement

Where a candidate you bring forward is successfully developed and placed into a Registered Manager opportunity through the pathway, the net profit or agreed placement fee is split equally between your organisation and the pathway agency.

The exact terms are agreed in writing before any candidate is formally accepted into the partnership route — ensuring transparency, fairness and a clear commercial understanding from the start.

Candidate & placement protection

All candidates entering through the provider partnership route agree that any Registered Manager role, interview, introduction or placement opportunity arising through the pathway is managed through the agency placement process, and must not bypass the pathway, the agency or the agreed partnership arrangement. This protects the investment made in the candidate's development and ensures every party is treated fairly and professionally.
